A system, running

Working schematic — drawn in code

This is Cairn — a job-search engine I'm building on the side. Below is its pipeline, drawn and animated in code — the same six-stage, fail-soft flow the real system runs. It scans employer job boards — applicant-tracking (ATS) platforms like Greenhouse and Lever — plus two aggregators, and when a source fails, it files a health entry and the scan keeps going. Hover any numbered box, or use the chips below, to inspect a stage.

Read the Cairn case study →

system standby

An animated schematic of Cairn's data pipeline, in six numbered stages. Stage 01, sources: four ATS boards — Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters — plus the Adzuna and JSearch aggregators. Stage 02, fail-soft adapters that report per-source health strings. Stage 03, normalize: jobs collapse to one canonical shape with content-hash identity. Stage 04, dedupe: the same role across sites becomes one card with a provenance trail. Stage 05, score: explainable fit in Strong, Good, and Stretch tiers. Stage 06, store and status events: one postings corpus fanning into per-profile matches over an append-only event stream. A failing source becomes an empty list plus a health entry — never a crashed scan.
